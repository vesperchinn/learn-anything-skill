## How to Verify

1. Check official documentation, textbooks, standards, or peer-reviewed papers.
2. Cross-reference with at least 2 independent sources for important claims.
3. Record the result in `claim_ledger.md`.
4. Update or remove verified items from this checklist.

## Verification Priority

- 🔴 **High**: Safety, security, legal, medical, financial, or certification topics.
- 🟡 **Medium**: Specific versions, APIs, dates, numbers, benchmark results, or technical specifications.
- 🟢 **Low**: General concepts, history, or well-established theory.
